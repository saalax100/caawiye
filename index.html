Nagu Saabsan

Ku soo dhawaada MOBILE HUB, oo ah xarunta ugu horreysa ee adeegyada casriga ah ee talefannada gacanta. Waxaan nahay khubaro ku takhasustay iibinta iyo cilad-bixinta dhammaan noocyada kala duwan ee mobile-lada sida Apple, Samsung, Infinix, Tecno, Oppo, Redmi, LG, Nokia, iyo kuwo kale oo badan.

Cilad-bixinta Hardware

Waxaan kuu haysanaa qalabkii ugu dambeeyay ee lagu farsameeyo matoorada mobile-lada, beddelista shaashadaha (Screens), xallinta ciladaha korontada (IC), iyo dayactirka guud ee hardware-ka.

Adeegyada Software

Khubaradayada software-ku waxay kuu qabanayaan ku shubista nidaamka (OS), furista qufulada (Unlocking), xallinta ciladaha system-ka, iyo cusboonaysiinta barnaamijyada.

Hadafkayagu waa inaan macaamiishayada u soo bandhigno adeeg tayo sare leh, dammaanad buuxda leh, iyo qiimo macquul ah. Haddii aad u baahan tahay inaad iibsato mobile cusub ama aad midkaaga cilad-ka-saarto, MOBILE HUB waa doorashadaada koowaad.
